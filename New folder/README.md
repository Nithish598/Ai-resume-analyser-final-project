## ⚡ Recommended PowerPoint Animations & Transitions

For maximum cinematic impact in Microsoft PowerPoint:

1. **Enable the Morph Transition**:
   - Go to `Transitions` ➔ Select `Morph` (Duration: `1.50` seconds).
   - Because character assets and glass cards share consistent naming and position logic, Morph creates fluid camera motion and holographic transformations between slides.
2. **Add Particle & Entrance Animations**:
   - For character images: Apply `Animations` ➔ `Float In` (Duration: `0.75s`, Smooth Start).
   - For gauges and progress bars: Apply `Wipe` (from Left) or `Wheel` (for circles).
3. **Presenter View**:
   - Every slide includes pre-written **Presenter Notes** with cinematic stage directions, transition cues, and timing.
